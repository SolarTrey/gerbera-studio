# Changelog

## 1.1 — November 2026

### New
- **Gerbera Labs.** A beaker button next to the rainbow in the **File** menu opens Labs, a home for experimental and just-for-fun extras that will grow over time. Each lab has its own on/off switch.
  - The beaker changes color with your theme: **mint** for Morning, **amber** for Evening, **violet** for Midnight.
  - Opening Labs plays a little **bubbling sound**. You can switch it off in Labs.
- **Labs: Nostalgic loading screen** (off by default). A glassy splash in the spirit of 2009-era installers that plays for about 10 seconds when Gerbera Studio starts, before the editor opens:
  - A real **sky photo that matches your theme**: bright clouds for Morning, a purple dusk for Evening, and the Milky Way for Midnight, with softly twinkling stars at night.
  - The Gerbera logo and a new **Outfit** wordmark, centered.
  - Status messages: *Initiating software… Booting up… Warming up the projector… Threading the film… Rolling…*
  - A silver glass progress bar with a gliding glow in **green**, **amber** or **violet** depending on the theme.
  - A **Preview** button in Labs, so you can see it without restarting.
- **Default export settings.** Click **Set as my default** in the Export dialog, or use **File ▸ Default export settings…**, to remember your favorite resolution, frame rate, video format, quality and audio. "My default" is then pre-selected every time you export.
- **What's new on update.** The first time you open Gerbera Studio after installing an update, a "What's new" window shows that version's changes. Fresh installs skip it.
- **New Split icon.** Split now shows two film frames with a cut line, so it's no longer confused with Cut's scissors.

### Changed
- **"Save movie" is now "Export movie"**, so it's clear which button saves your project and which makes a video file. The shortcut is still **Ctrl+E**.
- Updated the thank-you note behind the rainbow.

### Fixed
- **Green screen backgrounds now show up.** Keyed-out areas could appear black instead of showing your background image or video.
- Photos with transparent areas now display correctly.
- Fixed a startup crash related to the green-screen sliders.

## 1.0 — November 2026
- First release: storyboard editing, Morning / Evening / Midnight themes, 4K–8K export with H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9, ProRes and FFV1, GPU encoding, advanced frame rates, nostalgic and modern effects, green screen, reverse, audio effects matched to visuals, title styles and animations.
